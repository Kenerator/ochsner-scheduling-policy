# Use the scheduling assistant

Updated: 2026-10-09. Follow the [README setup and launch](../../README.md#setup). The interface identifies whether it uses live AI interpretation or explicit offline rehearsal. Use synthetic information only.

Ask for providers without identifying a patient. For booking, provide a supported specialty (primary care or dermatology), optional supported location (downtown, uptown or lakeside), phone and date of birth asYYYY-MM-DD. The assistant retains answers and asks only for missing information. If several records match, enter your synthetic ZIP privately; candidate records are not shown.

Choose a numbered returned appointment. Review its exact patient, provider, location and time, then reply **yes** to book or **no** to decline. A changed choice requires fresh confirmation. Only a service-confirmed booking is reported booked. Human guidance does not mean staff were contacted or a ticket queued.

**Reset** starts a conversation but preserves server bookings. An unknown booking outcome requires human reconciliation before retry; reset cannot resolve it. For no match, check phone/date of birth or use your usual scheduling contact. For service failure or unsupported work, follow the displayed next step. No medical advice, cancellation, rescheduling or existing-appointment retrieval is provided.
